FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from Deel?

Deel is built for enterprises with global workforces. Their pricing starts at $49/contractor/month. PayoutMate is built specifically for US SMBs managing domestic contractors, with flat monthly pricing regardless of contractor count.

Do my contractors pay any fees?

Zero. Unlike Upwork's 5-20% service fee, your contractors receive 100% of what you pay them. This helps you attract and retain better talent.

What about international contractors?

We're focused on US-based contractor payments and 1099 compliance right now. International payments are on our roadmap for 2025.

How does 1099 filing work?

We track all payments throughout the year, collect W-9s automatically, verify TINs with the IRS, and file 1099-NECs for all contractors who earned $600+ by the January deadline. You review and approve, we handle the rest.

Can I import my existing contractors?

Yes. Upload a CSV with names and emails, and we'll send W-9 requests automatically. Most teams are fully onboarded within 48 hours.

What integrations do you support?

QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Zapier (connecting to 5,000+ apps). Direct integrations with GoHighLevel, Shopify, and more coming soon.
